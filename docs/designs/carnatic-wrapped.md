## Problem Statement

A rasika attends 30+ concerts and several lecdems in Chennai every December season and logs each one: who performed (leads, accompanists by role, what each wore), where, and every item sung (composition, raga, tala, composer, language, topic). Today that happens in a phone note and on paper during the concert, then gets transcribed into a spreadsheet, where pivot tables become a hand-made "Wrapped" posted on social media.

Evidence (docs/reference/wrapped-2024-25.xlsx, the 2024-25 season):
- 33 concerts, 6 lecdems, 193 items (~6 per concert), 58 composers.
- Every concert date is blank; only lecdems have dates.
- Spelling variants split counts: Poorvikalyani / Purvikalyani, Shankarabharanam / Sankarabharanam, one composer as both "Tiruvettiyoor Tyagayya" and "Tiruvotriyur Thiyagaiyer".
- 40 cells are "?" or "-"; titles carry hedges like "Vararaga (?)". Uncertainty is part of how logging works.
- "Pantuvarali/Kamavardhini" appears as a hand-merged bucket: the raga name-pair problem.
- The Wrapped was built by hand: clothing colour frequency (white 23, red 15, blue 15), composer birth/death timeline (800 CE to living), top ragas, top topics (Rama 47, Devi 22), most-seen accompanist.

Carnatic Wrapped replaces the note, the paper and the spreadsheet with one phone app that works in halls with no signal, and makes the Wrapped a live, shareable view of the season.

## What Makes This Cool

- **No setlist source exists for Carnatic.** Western concert diaries (Concert Archives, setlist.fm, and the 2026 crop: Gigvault, Encore, Jukeboxd, Gigs) fetch setlists from the internet and all ship a "Wrapped". None model raga, tala, composer or deity. In Carnatic, the setlist exists only if the rasika captures it in the hall.
- **The Wrapped is the payoff, and it is live.** Stats redraw after every concert, so the card that grows through December is the reward for logging concert #24.
- **Statistics nobody else can compute:** the colours chart; a composer timeline spanning ~1,200 years; raga rarity against a 30k-song catalog ("you heard Kanakangi once; it appears in under 0.5% of kritis"); topics and deities.
- **Cover image idea (second opinion):** the season as a fabric. Each concert is a stripe in the colours the artists wore, ordered by date, threaded with raga names, lecdems woven differently. Redraws after every concert.

## Constraints

- **Deadline:** today is 27 Oct 2026. Logging must work on the phone by ~1 Dec 2026; Wrapped share cards by ~20 Dec (the season's peak runs mid-Dec to 1 Jan).
- **Connectivity:** sabha halls have poor or no mobile data. Nothing on the logging path may wait for the network.
- **Single user this season.** Friends are a later chapter; the data model must not prevent them.
- **Quality bar:** code quality, deliberate incremental decisions, tests first (see docs/skills-router.md and the constitution to come).
- **Catalog:** comes from the logger pipeline (/opt/carnatic/logger): song 30,539 · raga 1,326 · composer 589 · tala 77 · language 12 · subject 1,320 · form 14, with alias tables and raga name-pair groups. **Verified 27 Oct: the Supabase project no longer exists** (pooler returns "tenant/user not found"). The catalog is rebuildable on this machine: all 30,543 extracted records are in `pipeline/karnatik/extracted/` (132 MB), and Docker + Bun are installed, so `docker compose` (local Postgres) + `load.ts` + `resolve.ts` reproduce it. Those extracted files appear to exist only on this machine.
- **Catalog ids are not stable:** the logger's tables use identity columns and `resolve.ts` drops and recreates them every run. Only natural keys are stable: `song.kriti_id` (karnatik id), `composer.name_key`, the raga's canonical name and `group_key`, `subject.label`, tala and language names.
- **The catalog has no composer dates and no broad topics.** Composer rows carry no birth/death years; subjects are fine-grained (Kamakshi, Nataraja). Both the timeline and the topic roll-up need data owned by this project (see Recommended Approach).
- **Raga name-pair rule (user's dictum, recorded in the logger):** linked names (Pantuvarali ↔ Kamavardhini, Todi ↔ Hanumatodi) are never merged and never treated as unrelated. A song keeps its own name prominently, with the sibling shown small; analytics aggregate on the group.

## Premises

Agreed during the session (P4 revised after the second opinion):

1. **This season is the deadline; scope bends to fit.** Logging on the phone by ~1 Dec; Wrapped share cards from the same data before the season ends. Single user this season. *(Superseded 2026-10-29: the app is open to any rasika from 1 Dec; see specs/001-season-logger/spec.md Clarifications and constitution v1.1.0.)*
2. **The spreadsheet is the spec of record, not the June doc.** Capture: event (date, sabha, concert | lecdem), artists with roles and each artist's clothing colour, items in order (composition, raga, tala, composer, language, topic). No durations or segments in v1.
3. **Canonical entities, with "not sure" first-class.** Pick from the catalog so variants never split a count. "?" or free text is always allowed, kept as typed, and resolvable later; unresolved items still count.
4. **The schedule is an input, owned by the logger project.** This project defines the schedule format now and consumes the feed when it exists. A hand-built personal plan ("my plan": the concerts you intend to attend, entered ahead of time) guarantees pre-loaded logging whether or not the feed arrives.
5. **Offline-first is non-negotiable.** Everything needed to log lives on the phone; nothing on the logging path waits on the network; sync happens whenever signal returns.
6. **The Wrapped is a live view, not a year-end event.** Stats update after every concert. Shareable images (for Instagram) are a v1 deliverable, including the colours chart and composer timeline.

## Recommended Approach

**B: Local-first installable PWA + thin API + Postgres.** Chosen because it is the only option that is both offline and loss-proof, and the schedule feed and friends plug in later without re-architecture.

### Shape (architecture locked; specific libraries are for /speckit-plan to confirm)

- **Phone (the app):** an installable web app (PWA). The UI reads only from a local store on the device (IndexedDB). Every save is one local transaction that writes the record plus an outbox entry and returns immediately; no spinners on the write path.
- **Catalog snapshot:** a versioned, content-hashed export of the catalog (songs with raga/tala/composer/language/subject/form, ragas with name-pair groups, composers, talas) built from the logger's Postgres, precached by the service worker, never fetched per keystroke. Search uses one shared transliteration fold function on both index and query (Sahana/Saahana, Thyagaraja/Tyagaraja collapse to one key) plus the logger's alias tables.
- **Stable references:** the snapshot and every logged item reference catalog entries by natural key (`kriti_id`, composer `name_key`, raga canonical name, subject label, tala and language names), never by database id. Items never store a raga's name-pair group: the Wrapped looks up `group_key` from the current snapshot at aggregation time, so when the logger links or unlinks a name-pair, earlier items re-aggregate correctly (with a test that a regrouping in a new snapshot changes the top-ragas card for previously logged items). Each item also stores the text as typed or picked, as a fallback. Each snapshot version ships a remap of merged or retired keys, applied to stored items on upgrade; an item whose key disappears falls back to its stored text and lands in the unresolved list.
- **Project-owned reference data (this repo):** a composer-dates table seeded from the 2024-25 "Timelines" sheet (56 composers with birth/death years), extended by hand; composers without dates appear in an "undated" band on the timeline. A subject-to-topic roll-up table (e.g. Kamakshi → Devi, Nataraja → Shiva), seeded from the 2024-25 Topic column; items store the catalog subject, the topics card rolls up through the table, and unmapped subjects appear under their own label.
- **Push (outbox):** a small, single-flight sync loop pushes the outbox to the API on app start, on reconnect, on returning to the app and after each write. Idempotency keys prevent duplicates; ids are client-generated; last-write-wins per record. Deletes are soft (tombstone records synced like any write); item order is an explicit position field per item, synced as an update. **One writing device this season**; any other device is read-only.
- **Pull (feed, plan, restore):** the same loop pulls server changes since a server-issued cursor: schedule feed entries, "my plan" entries, and anything the server has that the phone lacks. Pulled schedule and plan entries are stored locally, so pre-loaded logging works with no signal. Rule: once you open, edit or mark an event attended, it is yours; later feed updates never overwrite it (they show as a "schedule changed" hint).
- **Restore:** two entry points. (1) **Empty device** (storage evicted along with the identity, reinstall, new phone, cleared site data): the first run finds no local data and no identity, shows sign-in (needs signal, once), then restores automatically before the logging screen appears. (2) **In place:** if a credential survives but the local store is empty, or you tap "Restore", it restores directly. Restore downloads the whole season (events, items, artists, sabhas, colours, plans, unresolved values). Any unsynced local outbox is pushed first, so a restore never discards a local write.
- **API + Postgres:** a thin TypeScript API in front of Postgres, next to (or reading from) the catalog. Stores events, items, artists, colours, plans; serves the schedule feed and catalog snapshot versions.
- **Auth:** one user. The cached identity is enough offline; a network error never sends you to a login screen; a long-lived session.
- **Loss protection:** request persistent storage on first run; the app is used from the home screen (installed); sync on every reconnect; restore from the server; one-tap export (JSON, plus xlsx shaped like the 2024-25 sheet).
- **Install size:** the 2 MB first-install budget covers the app shell, catalog snapshot and search index. Card-rendering assets (rendering wasm, embedded fonts) and the xlsx writer are a second precache phase, fetched right after install with their own budget of 3 MB. The app shows "ready for offline" only after both phases are cached, so you know before walking into a hall.
- **Wrapped:** computed on the phone from local data, so it works offline. Share cards render to PNG on the device. Card set for this season: season fabric cover, colours, top ragas (name-pair aware), composer timeline, topics, most-seen artists and accompanists, sabhas, firsts and rarities.

### Capture model (v1)

- **Event:** date, start time, sabha, kind (concert | lecdem | other, e.g. dance), status (planned | attended), source (schedule feed | my plan | created on the spot), optional note.
- **Sabhas** are local entities with client-generated ids: seeded from the 2024-25 sheet (11 sabhas) and from feed entries, picked rather than typed, each with aliases, with a merge action for duplicates. A feed sabha value matches a sabha by name or alias; an unmatched value creates a new sabha flagged for review.
- **Artists** are local entities with client-generated ids: created on first entry, picked thereafter (autosuggest from your own history plus the ~100 names in the 2024-25 sheet), with aliases and a merge action for duplicates. Later reconciliation with the logger's artist catalog is Open Question 3.
- **People on stage:** artist + role (lead vocal/instrument, second lead, violin, mridangam, khanjira, ghatam, morsing, other) + clothing colour: a primary colour plus an optional secondary, from a fixed swatch palette. The colours chart counts both colours of a two-tone outfit.
- **Item (ordered by an explicit position):** composition picked from the catalog, which auto-fills raga, tala, composer, language and subject; every field can be overridden or set to "?"; optional note. Special forms without a catalog song: RTP (raga or ragas, tala, optional theme), tani avartanam, viruttam/slokam, thillana by an unlisted composer, free text. Ragamalika keeps its list of ragas.
- **Lecdem:** title + presenters (up to 3) + date + sabha; items optional.
- **Unresolved values** are stored exactly as typed, count in the Wrapped as their own bucket, and can be resolved later from a simple list.

### Schedule contract (defined here, delivered by the logger project)

A versioned feed of planned performances: id, date, start time, sabha, kind, artists with roles, title (for lecdems), source and last-updated. "My plan" entries use the same shape, so logging starts from a pre-filled event either way. Exact format to be written in /speckit-plan as a contract file.

## Success Criteria

Functional, by 1 Dec 2026:
- A full concert (event, 5 artists with roles and colours, 8 items, event and item notes) logged on the phone in airplane mode; the app force-closed and reopened; nothing lost. After reconnecting, everything reaches the server within 30 s with no duplicates.
- Restore: with a synced season on the server, clear the app's site data, reopen, sign in; the full season (events, items, artists, sabhas, colours, plans) is back before the logging screen appears.
- An item deleted and two items reordered offline show the same result on the server after sync.
- Starting from a planned concert (feed or my plan), logging needs no typing for date, sabha or artists.
- Last season's 193 items imported through the same resolver: at least 90% resolve to a catalog song or a deliberate "?" without manual cleanup (fixture; target to be confirmed once measured).

Performance (mid-range phone, installed app):
- Cold start to the logging screen in airplane mode under 1.5 s.
- Catalog search under 50 ms p95 from keystroke to results, offline.
- A local save under 100 ms; no spinner on any save.
- Shell + catalog + search index under 2 MB compressed on first install; card-rendering assets and the xlsx writer under a further 3 MB, cached before "ready for offline" shows.
- No logout after 30 days without network.
- At least 95% of a golden set of ~100 variant-spelling queries return the right song in the top 3.

Wrapped, by ~20 Dec:
- Share cards render offline to PNG at Instagram story size, and reflect the latest concert within a second of saving it.
- The 2024-25 colours chart and composer timeline can be reproduced from imported data.
