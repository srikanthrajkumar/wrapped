## Problem Statement

The Season page is effectively the app; Wrapped is the flex on top. On the Maintainer's iPhone
(2026-11-09) Season read as "a lot of distraction, so many events, but no focus. Not sense that
I've found the right thing." Two redesigns of its structure the same day (a scrolling timeline
with a strip of days, then one day at a time in Morning, Afternoon and Evening bands) did not fix
it. The second was built and withdrawn on sight: "the issue is definitely not that they don't
have a sense of time. The Now etc. was enough." The time line and the highlighted card were not
wanted.

The missing piece was the job. A regular sabha-hopper decides through the day, on the move, not
in the morning: "It is 3:40, and say we are at Academy, we decide that the concert we want to
attend needs to get viewed at 4pm." Competing choices are normal ("It is not a bug that we have
competing choices of places we want to go, and that we didn't plan this in the morning"). The
pick turns on distance, or on the accompanists. Today that happens on chennaievents, whose only
gaps are a "mid UI and clunky phone interface" and no logging; in the evening "I tend to have to
scroll all the way down … just to find the concerts I can go to." And once a concert is logged,
"which I logged doesn't have a place to go."

## What Makes This Cool

Festival apps we found (Clashfinder Pal, PocketSets, Setline, Newport Folk's) offer "now and
next" as a mode beside a full-day timetable, because festival-goers plan favourites in
advance. A sabha-hopper doesn't; the pick happens at the door.
So here the now-and-next answer is the home screen, and logging (not a booking or a plan) is the
moment a concert becomes yours. The whole app becomes three stages of a concert: what's on,
interested, heard. Wrapped stops being only stats: its tab holds every concert you heard, with the stats on top.

## Constraints

- Constitution: offline-first logging (I), nothing lost (II), the rasika decides (III), grounded
  in the data (IV), size and latency budgets (V), one decision at a time by the Maintainer (VI).
- Dark is the default theme; restrained palette; few on-screen words (DESIGN.md).
- Data: the Schedule feed (542 entries in 2026-27, billing with role hints) and the rasika's own
  Events. No artist photos exist in either.
- **Copy is not approved.** Every word on the mockups (labels, the hero's clash line, tile text)
  is a placeholder to be settled separately.

## Premises (agreed)

1. Season's job is the pick made on the move, at any hour: what can I still catch, and which is
   worth it. Planning ahead is secondary.
2. What's already over today is noise; concerts under way are marked Now.
3. The pick turns on who's on stage (lead and accompanists) and where; for a regular the sabha's
   name says where.
4. (Revised during the session) A concert is identified by its type, not a photo.
5. One Interested, not maybe-then-plan; logging is the commitment (named on 2026-11-10; it was
   "Save" in the session).
6. chennaievents already has the data; the gap is the phone UI and logging.

## The design, in parts

Each part stands alone, so each can be accepted, changed or dropped on its own. Decision
numbers here (SF-D1 to SF-D20) are this session's, asked in order on 2026-11-09; they are not
DESIGN.md's or plan.md's D-numbers.

### Part 1. The spine: Listed → Interested → Heard (settled 2026-11-10)

- **What:** three stages a concert passes through for one rasika. Listed (on the Schedule),
  Interested (you'd like to go, maybe or surely), Heard (you logged it). "Planned" goes as a
  separate state: the listing's "Add" becomes Interested, and the Event's "Start logging" goes.
- **Why:** the Maintainer, on seeing Saved beside Add and Start logging: "we now have various
  entrypoints before logging." The hopper commits once, at the door. Maybe and going are the
  same act until you walk in; walking in and logging is the commitment.
- **The name (SF-D23):** "Interested", the word Songkick, Facebook Events and Resident Advisor
  use for "I'd like to go", chosen over "Want to hear" and "Saved". It should feel like "a list of
  things I have expressed an interest to go to." Exact copy is still to be approved.
- **What it writes (SF-D21):** Interested is today's Planned Event under a new name: the same
  record, sync, "schedule changed" hints and one-id-per-attempt retries (ADR 0007). Every Event
  stored as Planned today, on phones and on the server, becomes Interested with the same id and
  source, on upgrade and through sync and restore (constitution II). An ADR records the change
  before code depends on it.
- **Undoing it (SF-D22):** the same button in the listing's sheet toggles it off, from What's
  on or from Interested; underneath it deletes that Event, synced, with Undo. Once Items are
  logged the concert is Heard and the button is gone; deleting it goes through the Event's menu,
  as today.
- **How logging begins (SF-D25):** the listing's sheet holds Interested and a second button
  (wording to approve, for example "I'm here") that makes the Event and opens it ready for its
  first Item: the walk-in's one tap. An Interested concert, or the hero's Now, opens straight to
  Add Item. The first Item logged makes it Heard. The + still starts a Concert not on the
  Schedule straight into logging.
- **When its time passes unlogged (SF-D24, SF-D39):** an Interested concert lapses when its
  logging window ends (four hours after its start). The next time the app opens, after any
  Concert summary, one card on Home lists that day's lapsed ones, asking whether you were at any
  (wording to approve). Each one tapped goes to Heard, Items optional; the rest drop from view.
  One in the same slot as a concert you logged drops without being asked. This is the "Did you
  go?" prompt the product design deferred (T056).
- **Missed (SF-D38):** a dropped one becomes Missed, a status the app keeps but no longer shows
  or counts: still in Export, sync and backups, so nothing is lost (constitution II). Events
  already stored as Missed stay exactly as they are.
- **The stored value:** whether `planned` is renamed in storage or kept with the new word on
  screen (with its schema-version and sync consequences) is the ADR's call.
- **"I'm here" left with nothing logged:** taken back as an untouched Concert from the + is
  (screens/new-concert.ts); an Interested concert opens ready for Add Item only inside its
  logging window, and otherwise shows its Event as today.
- **Paths it serves:** planner (Interested in the morning, logged in the evening); hopper
  (Interested in several, picks one at the door, logs it); walk-in ("I'm here" from the listing);
  not on the Schedule (the +); didn't go (one quiet question, then gone).

### Part 2. Home, a hub of small things (settled 2026-11-10)

- **What:** the home opens with the hero (as today, on the motif), then "Starting soon", a link
  to all of today, then a tile for Interested, one line of what's new in your Season, and the
  concert you last heard. Settings moves to a small mark on Home. The search bar stays at the
  bottom.
- **Why:** the Maintainer's idea: "a little like uber … Design the main page as a set of many
  things in small. Some stats, some next some other details," keeping "the existing semblance of
  character." The 3:40 pick must be answered on open (SF-D16, option A); the full list is one tap.
- **Starting soon (SF-D26, SF-D27):** concerts under way, then the next slot: the next start
  time and any within 30 minutes of it, however far off (at 4:10 it shows the 6:00 to 6:30 slot,
  never nothing). Only concerts under way or in the next slot qualify. Up to four rows: your
  Interested ones among them first, then the next slot, then other concerts under way, then a
  count of the rest ("+8 more") into What's on (SF-D37). Each row is Part 4's row.
- **Search (SF-D28):** the bar sits on Home and What's on. A pick opens What's on filtered, the
  strip keeping only that name's days, as today. The kind toggles stay; Mine goes, since
  Interested is its own tab.
- **Your Season in small (SF-D29, SF-D40):** a block labelled Wrapped, opening the Wrapped tab:
  your last concert, then a small graph of your Season. The bare line of what's new ("Kalyani
  for the fourth time") was dropped: unlabelled under Interested it read as part of Interested.
  The graph's design is deferred ("a little more polished. Like an actual graph").
- **Replaces:** the Season page as one long timeline from today on.

### Part 3. What's on (the full list, one tap from Home; settled 2026-11-10)

- **What:** today from now on: concerts under way, labelled Now, with when they started; those
  about to start; then later today. What's over today folds to one line. Other days are a tap
  away on the strip of days, which starts at Today (SF-D32): Past goes, a day gone being two taps
  away (the calendar, then the day) for a late log. A search that matches only days gone says
  so, with the calendar to reach them.
- **Why:** "It is annoying that on chennai events, I tend to have to scroll all the way down in
  the evening just to find the concerts I can go to. Yes, the ongoing ones should also be logged
  as 'Now'." Hoppers walk into concerts already under way.
- **Under way (SF-D30):** a concert reads Now from its start until 2½ hours after it, then folds
  away as over; the same rule for listings and the rasika's own rows, except that an own
  concert still being logged (the logging hold) keeps reading Now and stays unfolded, so the
  list never disagrees with the hero. The feed's slots (the
  Academy's 9:00, 11:45, 1:30, 4:00, 6:45) sit 1¾ to 2¾ hours apart, 2½ to 2¾ for most. An Event's logging window
  stays as today (an hour before its start to four hours after; `WINDOW_BEFORE_MS` and
  `WINDOW_AFTER_MS` in app/src/store/logging-note.ts), so a late Item is never cut off.
- **Your own rows (SF-D31):** in start-time order with everyone else's, with a quiet mark rather
  than bold or a card: the Interested mark, Now while being logged, the Item count once heard. A
  cancelled one stays in its slot, marked, as today. Home's Starting soon is where yours come
  first.
- **Replaces:** the list starting at the first concert of the morning, and own-first ordering.
- **Evidence:** festival apps reached the same answer: Newport Folk's app was built around
  "who's playing now, who's playing next" (cantina.co/work/newport-folk-festival); a Coachella
  redesign added a live marker because testers couldn't tell live from upcoming
  (contra.com/p/ON3BFPbg-coachella-app-redesign).

### Part 4. The row: lead, type, accompanists, sabha and time

- **What:** each listing row shows the lead artist, the concert's type when it isn't vocal
  (Violin, Flute, Veena, Jugalbandi; Harikatha, Lecdem and Dance keep their marks), the
  accompanists on a muted line, then the sabha and the time. No faces. No per-row button.
- **Why:** the pick is "by distance, and at others because of the accompaniments", and the
  sabha's name already carries distance for a regular (SF-D3). Faces were tried and replaced by type
  (SF-D12): photos for hundreds of artists would need a source, and type comes free from the
  billing.
- **Rule behind the counts (a first cut, to be recorded in the ADR):** leads are the billed
  Artists whose role is not an accompanying one (violin, mridangam, ghatam, khanjira, morsing,
  tabla, thavil, nattuvangam, vocal support), else the first billed; the type is their role;
  two leads of different kinds are a Jugalbandi; a Group reads by its group name. Violin is the
  type only when no other lead is billed. A concert whose billing carries no roles at all (or
  bills nobody) can't be typed and shows no type, exactly as a vocal one does. This differs from
  the existing known-by rule (which leaves violin out of the name) only in naming a type, never
  the title.
- **Evidence (the 2026-27 feed as published 2026-11-08, 542 entries; 415 of them concerts, the
  rest 114 Dances, 9 Harikathas and 4 Lecdems; counted with the rule above):** 276 vocal (67%),
  so a "Vocal" mark on most rows says nothing; 22 violin, 15 veena, 9 flute, 5 mandolin, 3
  chitravina, 3 nagaswaram, 5 other single leads (presenter, mridangam, other); 14 with two leads
  of different kinds (flute with veena, and others), the Jugalbandis; 63 (15%) with no roles to
  read, showing no type. 276 + 22 + 15 + 9 + 5 + 3 + 3 + 5 + 14 + 63 = 415.
- **Constitution:** the type is worked out on screen from the billing's role hints and never
  stored, so no data shape changes (VI) and it never overrides the rasika's own Event (III).
- **Open:** the type marks themselves (words or icons), deferred by the Maintainer as "a later
  activity"; whether the accompanist line earns its height (SF-D4 was deferred until seen, and is
  now in the mockup).

### Part 5. Interested, in the listing's sheet

- **What:** a tap on a row opens its sheet (the full billing, as now) with Interested and "I'm
  here". A row you're interested in carries a small mark by its time (the star in the mockup);
  the rest carry nothing.
- **Why:** a star on every row was "way too big and it repeating is kinda jarring" (SF-D13).
- **Replaces:** Add on the listing sheet.
- **Settled with Part 1:** the sheet holds Interested and "I'm here" (SF-D25); Interested
  toggles off in place (SF-D22).

### Part 6. Interested

- **What:** a tab listing the concerts you're interested in by time, today first. Interested concerts
  starting within 30 minutes of each other read as one slot (SF-D33).
- **Why:** hoppers shortlist several and choose at the door (SF-D6), and the shortlist needs one
  tap from anywhere, an Event included (SF-D20).
- **Evidence:** PocketSets flags saved sets that clash (its App Store listing); here a clash
  is simply one slot with two rows.
- **Settled with Part 1:** past ones don't stay: each is asked about once, then goes (SF-D24).

### Part 7. The hero (settled 2026-11-10)

- **What:** the concert you're logging now; else an Interested concert under way (it reads Now
  for its 2½ hours, a tap opening it ready for the first Item; SF-D34); else the soonest you're
  interested in. A hand-made Event (the +) counts as it does in today's build, as an own Event
  under way or ahead; a cancelled one never does; with
  nothing to show, the hero falls back to the "Season" title. Logging any other concert takes the
  hero at once.
- **A clash (SF-D33):** two Interested concerts in the same slot, starting within 30 minutes of
  each other (4:00 and 4:30 clash; 4:00 and 6:00 don't), the same slot rule as Starting soon and
  the Interested list. The hero then reads as a choice between them, named for the slot rather
  than the clock (the Maintainer's example: "something like pick your dusk").
- **Why:** the hero must never pick for you (SF-D19); this morning's bug was the hero naming one
  concert while the list showed another.
- **Open:** the copy, and the slot names (morning, dusk, evening…).

### Part 8. Wrapped: what you heard, and your Season (SF-D41)

- **What:** a tab of every concert you've heard, newest first, with your Season so far
  (concerts, Items, ragas, sabhas, most heard) at its top: today's Wrapped.
- **Why:** "The big trouble with status quo is that which I logged doesn't have a place to go"
  (SF-D18). The stats describe exactly these concerts, so one place holds both.
- **Replaces:** Wrapped as its own tab; heard concerts living only in past days of the list.
- **Committed:** the shareable Cards and their Share (a v1 deliverable in the product design)
  live under the Season-so-far block at its top.
- **The name (SF-D41):** the tab keeps the name Wrapped ("let's call in wrapped not history");
  it now holds every concert you heard as well as the stats.
- **Open:** how much of the current Wrapped screen moves in (Home's line is settled, SF-D29).

### Part 9. The tab bar: Home · Interested · Wrapped

- **What:** three tabs, one per stage of a concert. Settings moves to a small mark on Home.
- **Why:** at 3:40 you flip between Home and Interested; Wrapped is the flex (SF-D20).
- **Replaces:** Season · Wrapped · Settings.
- **Committed:** Settings' mark takes the amber "something to fix" state whenever a Settings row
  is amber, and Export stays reachable from it (constitution II).
- **Committed:** the rows Season carries above its content today keep their rules and move to
  the top of Home, under the hero: the install row (keeping FB-46's one place on every tab; in a
  browser tab Home's hero yields to the plain title, as Season's does today), "Getting ready for offline…", the new-catalog prompt, the sync status line, and the
  sign-in and other-phone lines.

**One switch.** The core lands as one change, merged when it passes, the migration inside it,
so the live app never runs half the old Season and half the new (builder's call: a single
change, not a feature flag, since the data change and the screens must move together). Order
of work inside it: Part 1's ADR and data change first, then Parts 3 and 4, then 5 to 7, then
the tabs.

### R2-1 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

> Part 4's new breakdown of the 415 concerts does not add up. Inputs: 276 vocal + 20 violin + 15 veena + 9 flute + 5 mandolin + 3 chitravina + 9 other single leads + 17 Jugalbandis + 63 untyped. Operation: sum. Result: 417, two more than the 415 concerts stated in the same sentence. The base itself checks (415 + 114 Dances + 9 Harikathas + 4 Lecdems = 542), and so do the percentages (276 / 415 = 66.5%, about 67%; 63 / 415 = 15.2%). But some concerts are counted twice, or a category count is wrong. These counts are the evidence for Part 4's type rule and for dropping the Vocal mark.

**Remedy**

> Recount against the 2026-11-08 feed with the recorded rule. Correct the category counts so they sum to 415, or explain the overlap, for example concerts counted both as a single lead and as a Jugalbandi.

### R2-2 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

> The recorded type rule (leads are billed Artists whose role is not violin, mridangam, ghatam, khanjira, morsing, tabla or nattuvangam; two leads of different kinds are a Jugalbandi) leaves thavil and vocal support (the `support` role in data-model.md) off the accompanying list. Under the rule, every nagaswaram concert billed with its thavil becomes a 'Jugalbandi'. The design's own evidence confirms this: '17 have two leads of different kinds (nagaswaram with thavil ...), the Jugalbandis'. Thavil is the nagaswaram's accompaniment, so this is not a jugalbandi. Likewise a vocalist billed with vocal support would read as a Jugalbandi of vocal and support. Rows would carry a wrong type label, and the Jugalbandi count of 17 (and so R2-1's sum) rests on it.

**Remedy**
