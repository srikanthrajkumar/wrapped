Historical material to draw on when writing the State of Rasikatvam report. Each note keeps its
source and page numbers so it can be cited. The numbering follows the Maintainer's own notes.

## 2. Radio play counts, 1931–32

**Source:** pp. 187–188 (source title to add). The Madras Corporation Broadcasting report counts
how often each rāga was performed on air.

- **The numbers:**
  - Kalyāṇi tops the list at 125.
  - Next come Śaṅkarābharaṇam, Harikāmbhōji, Bhairavi, Khamās, Kāmbhōji, Hamsadhvani, Jhañjhūṭi
    and Surati, then Pantuvarāḷi and Aṭāṇā (tied).
  - Dvijāvanti, Hamsānandi, Lathāṅgi and Nāgasvarāvaḷi were each heard only once.
- **The Academy's role:** it helped "overcome the prejudice some of the professional musicians
  had against singing on the Radio".

*Possible use:* a 1931–32 baseline for the season's top Ragas. Read it beside the rasikas' own
counts, which come from the Wrapped's top-Ragas Card, aggregated across rasikas only for those
who consented to season reports.
